**This plugin is under development.**

The Coupons plugin will allow defining and applying discount codes to subscription plans.

Planned features:

- Fixed and percentage discount codes
- Time-limited promotional campaigns
- First-payment and recurring discount types
- Coupon usage tracking and limits
